# AgriSleuth — Track 2

Researchers do not just need a likely disease name. They need to know which observations support it, which sources disagree, and what to verify next.

AgriSleuth investigates rice and maize symptoms using cited disease evidence. Its local-model Controller chooses tools, tests tentative hypotheses with a Critic and deterministic Judge, and either produces a sourced explanation or asks a focused question. The report keeps uncertainty visible instead of fabricating confidence.

Zetaris provides the real evidence-query layer. Meterless Markovian bounds the investigation and preserves checkpoints. The interface shows actual source IDs, tool decisions, criticisms and stopping reasons.

This is research support, not confirmed diagnosis or treatment advice. Current evidence covers 26 diseases. Live model behavior and software tests are reported separately; biological accuracy has not been established.
